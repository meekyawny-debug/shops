## Best Posting Times

### Why 10 AM, 12 PM, and 6 PM ET

These three time slots are based on aggregated Meta/Instagram engagement data and industry research:

| Time Slot | Rationale | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **10:00 AM ET** | Morning scroll. Users check Instagram during commute, coffee break, or start of workday. High reach for discovery-type content. | Product spotlights, new arrivals, trending roundups |
| **12:00 PM ET** | Lunch break peak. Engagement rates spike as users browse during midday downtime. Highest save and share rates. | Educational content, guides, infographics, tips |
| **6:00 PM ET** | Evening wind-down. Users are off work and leisurely scrolling. Longest average session time. Best for emotional or aspirational content. | Quotes, engagement posts, lifestyle imagery, sales |

### Weekend Bonus Posting Opportunities

| Day | Optimal Window | Content Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturday** | 10:00 AM -- 11:00 AM ET | Behind-the-scenes Stories, casual Reels, UGC reposts |
| **Sunday** | 5:00 PM -- 7:00 PM ET | "Week ahead" teasers, self-care content, mood boards |

Weekend posts are optional but recommended during product launches, sales events, or when a Reel is performing well and a follow-up can ride the momentum. Instagram engagement on weekends tends to be slightly lower in volume but higher in depth (more saves, longer video watch times) according to Meta's Creator Insights reports.

### Platform-Specific Notes

- **Reels** perform best when posted between 9 AM and 12 PM ET on weekdays (Meta Business Suite data, 2025).
- **Carousel posts** see peak engagement at 12 PM and 6 PM ET when users have time to swipe.
- **Stories** should be posted 1--2 hours before or after a feed post to drive traffic to the main content.

## Content Mix Strategy

The following ratio ensures a healthy balance between selling, educating, and building community:

| Content Type | Target % | Post Types | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product** | 40% | Spotlights, new arrivals, detail shots, back-in-stock | Drive traffic and conversions |
| **Educational / Tips** | 30% | Routines, styling guides, checklists, how-tos | Build authority and trust |
| **Engagement** | 20% | Quotes, polls, this-or-that, trending roundups | Grow reach and community |
| **Promotional** | 10% | Flash sales, discount codes, limited-time offers | Generate urgency and revenue |

### Hashtag Rotation Strategy

To avoid Instagram flagging repetitive hashtag use:

1. **Never copy-paste the same full set twice in a row.** Rotate at least 5 hashtags between posts.
2. **Use the 15-20 rule.** Pull 15-20 from your bank per post, mixing categories each time.
3. **Lead with 2-3 branded hashtags** on every post for discoverability.
4. **Include 3-5 broad hashtags** (100K-1M posts) for reach.
5. **Include 5-7 niche hashtags** (10K-100K posts) for targeted discovery.
6. **Include 2-3 micro hashtags** (under 10K posts) unique to the post topic.
7. **Place hashtags in the first comment**, not the caption, for a cleaner look (Instagram indexes both equally).

## Notes and Guidelines

### Content Creation Checklist

Before publishing any post, verify:

- [ ] Caption includes a clear CTA (shop, save, comment, tag, link in bio)
- [ ] Line breaks are used for readability (no walls of text)
- [ ] Hashtags are placed in the first comment (15-20 per post)
- [ ] Image/video meets Instagram's recommended specs (1080x1080 feed, 1080x1920 Reels/Stories)
- [ ] Alt text is added to every image for accessibility
- [ ] Product is tagged via Instagram Shopping (if applicable)
- [ ] Link in bio is updated to match the current post's CTA
- [ ] Story is posted 1-2 hours before or after to drive traffic to the feed post
- [ ] Post is scheduled at the correct brand-specific time slot

### Engagement Protocol (First 30 Minutes After Posting)

1. Reply to the first 10 comments within 15 minutes to boost algorithmic ranking.
2. Post 2-3 Stories referencing the feed post (poll, question sticker, or "new post" alert).
3. Engage with 10-15 accounts in your niche (comment on their posts, not just likes).
4. Share the post to relevant hashtag Stories if applicable.
