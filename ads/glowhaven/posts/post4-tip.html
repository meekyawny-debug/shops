The Power of Snail Mucin
Snail mucin contains glycoproteins, hyaluronic acid, and glycolic acid -- a natural powerhouse that helps skin retain moisture up to 3x longer than regular moisturizers.

Fun fact: Snail mucin has been used in skincare since ancient Greece, where Hippocrates reportedly recommended crushed snails for skin inflammation.
